Software that fits how you already work.

Enquiry trackers, billing, booking, stock and staff tools, and dashboards that show your numbers at a glance, built for your business instead of bought off the shelf.

What we build

  • Enquiry and lead trackers: every enquiry from ads, WhatsApp, calls and walk-ins in one place, with follow-up dates and who is handling it
  • Billing and GST invoices, with payment and due-amount tracking
  • Booking and appointments, with reminders to customers and a day view for staff
  • Stock and orders for shops, distributors and manufacturers
  • Staff tools: attendance, daily tasks, targets and incentive tracking
  • Dashboards that show sales, collections, enquiries and ad spend on one screen, on your phone or computer

Start from the spreadsheets you already have

Most businesses already run on Excel or Google Sheets. Often the fastest win is to keep those sheets and automate around them: daily summaries, follow-up reminders, reports that build themselves and data entered once instead of three times. When a sheet is no longer enough, we move the data into a proper app without losing anything.

Connected to what you already use

Your website forms, WhatsApp, Google Sheets, email and ad accounts can feed into one system, so enquiries and sales are recorded automatically instead of copied by hand.

Built with us, owned by you

We start by watching how your staff work today and agree the screens with you before building. You review everything on a preview link, we test it on your devices and show your team how to use it. The software and its data belong to you.

For G-TEC Computer Education we built an enquiry form that records which ad each lead came from, and a lobby TV attendance system that became our product BatchBoard. Need a phone or desktop app instead? See App Development.

Questions

Why custom software instead of an off-the-shelf app?

Off-the-shelf software makes you change how you work, and you keep paying for features you never use. Custom software does only what your business needs, the way your staff already do it.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. We build it to run in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, and as an installed app where that makes more sense.

Can you import our existing Excel data?

Yes. We bring in your existing customer, product or sales data, so you start with your full history.

Who looks after it once it is built?

We do, if you want us to. We fix problems and make changes as your business grows, and we agree how that works before the build starts.

Tell us what you sell and who buys it.

We'll tell you what would bring you more enquiries or save your team time: a website, ads, creatives, automation or an app. No obligation.
