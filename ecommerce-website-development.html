Sell online. Get paid by UPI.

Online stores that take UPI, card and cash-on-delivery payments, load fast on mobile data and show up on Google.

What your store includes

  • Product catalogue with categories, search, photos, sizes and variants
  • Cart and checkout that works well on a phone, which is where most of your customers will order
  • Online payments through a payment gateway such as Razorpay, PhonePe or Cashfree: UPI, cards, net banking and wallets, plus cash on delivery if you offer it
  • GST-inclusive prices and invoices with your GST details
  • Delivery charges set by pincode, district or order value
  • Order alerts to your staff, and order confirmations to the customer by email and WhatsApp
  • An orders and stock page for your team, where you can add products, change prices and mark items out of stock

Built to be found, not just to look good

A store nobody finds doesn't sell. Every store gets the same SEO work as our websites: a page for each category written for what people actually search, Product structured data so Google can show price and stock, a sitemap, and a product feed for Google Merchant Center so your products can appear in Google's free shopping listings.

Start simple with WhatsApp ordering

Not ready for online payments? We can build the catalogue first, with an order button that writes the customer's cart into a WhatsApp message to you. You confirm the order and payment the way you do now, and add a payment gateway later without rebuilding the store.

Your store, in your name

The domain, the payment gateway account and the store itself stay in your name. There is no store-builder subscription. You pay for hosting and the payment gateway's own transaction fee, which we explain before you sign up.

Questions

Which payment methods can my customers use?

Whatever your payment gateway supports, usually UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets. Cash on delivery can be added as a separate option.

Can I add products and change prices myself?

Yes. The store comes with a page where your team adds products, changes prices and marks items out of stock, without touching code.

Do I need a payment gateway account?

Yes, for online payments. The gateway account is opened in your business's name, and money from sales goes straight to your bank account. We help with the set-up and connect it to the store.

Can you add online ordering to my existing website?

Usually, yes. We look at your current site first and tell you whether to add a store section or build the store separately.
