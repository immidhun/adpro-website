Home / Website Maintenance & Hosting

Your website, kept fast, safe and up to date.

Hosting, domain and SSL renewals, backups, content updates and monthly SEO checks, handled for you. We look after sites we built and sites someone else built.

What we take care of

  • Hosting set up and managed, with free SSL so your site shows the padlock
  • Domain and SSL renewals tracked, so your site never goes down because a renewal was missed
  • Backups of your site, so it can be restored if anything goes wrong
  • Content updates: new prices, offers, photos, staff, courses or whole new pages
  • Speed and broken-link checks, so pages keep loading fast on mobile data
  • Google Search Console checks for pages Google can't index, and the searches people find you with
  • Google Business Profile updates: hours, photos and posts kept in line with your website
  • Business email on your own domain, set up and connected

A short report every month

Each month we tell you in a few lines what we checked, what we changed, how many people found you on Google and anything that needs your decision. No jargon and no padding.

Taking over a site someone else built

Your old developer has disappeared? We take over WordPress, Wix and page-builder sites as well as hand-coded ones. We check the site, secure the logins and domain in your name, fix what is broken, and tell you plainly whether it is worth keeping. If a rebuild makes more sense, we do it without losing your Google rankings. Read how we rebuild a website without losing rankings.

Your accounts stay yours

The domain, hosting and Google accounts are kept in your business's name, with us added as a partner. If you ever move on, everything stays with you.
