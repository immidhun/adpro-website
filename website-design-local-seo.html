Hand-coded, fast-loading business websites built to be found on Google, not just to look nice.

Why hand-coded, not a page builder

Most small-business sites in Kerala are drag-and-drop builder templates. They load slowly on mobile data, every business looks the same, and the SEO settings are shallow. We write every page by hand as plain, static HTML.

  • Loads fast on 4G: no page-builder scripts, no plugins.
  • Nothing to hack or update: there is no WordPress admin or plugin to patch.
  • Cheap to host: a whole site is usually around a megabyte.
  • Every page written for a search: its own title, description and structured data.

What comes with every website

Local SEO is not an add-on. Every site we deliver ships with the same package:

  • On-page and technical SEO: titles, descriptions, headings, sitemap, robots, redirects, HTTPS
  • Schema.org structured data: LocalBusiness, Service or Product, FAQ, breadcrumbs
  • Google Business Profile setup or clean-up
  • NAP consistency: your name, address and phone the same everywhere
  • Local citations and a review strategy
  • Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 connected
  • WhatsApp enquiry button and forms that open a ready-written WhatsApp message

Locality and service pages

People search for “home nurse in Koramangala” or “water bottle supplier Kottayam”, not for your company name. So we build a page for each service you sell and each area you cover, each written for that search, so you can show up for it.

Moving from an old site without losing Google

If you already have a site that ranks, we list every existing address before touching anything. Pages keep their URL wherever possible and the rest get permanent (301) redirects, so the rankings you have already earned carry over.

Questions

Can you rebuild my existing website?

Yes. We pull your current sitemap first, keep the addresses that already rank, and redirect the rest, so a rebuild doesn't reset your Google position.

Do I need WordPress?

No. Our sites are static HTML, so there's no WordPress admin, plugin updates or database to maintain. Content changes are made by us and re-uploaded.

Will my business show on Google Maps?

We set up or clean up your Google Business Profile and keep your name, address and phone identical across the site and listings. That consistency is what Maps ranking relies on.

Where is the website hosted?

On fast shared hosting with free SSL. We can use your existing hosting and domain, or arrange new ones for you.
