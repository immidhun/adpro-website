More direct bookings, less commission.

Booking websites, Google visibility, reels and ads for homestays, houseboats, resorts and Ayurveda stays, from a team based in Alappuzha.

What homestays, houseboats & resorts need online

  • A website guests trust enough to book on directly
  • Real availability and online payment, so guests don't have to wait for a reply
  • To show up on Google Maps and Google search, not only on booking apps
  • Photos and reels that show the place honestly
  • Fast answers to guests' questions in English, Malayalam and other languages
  • Fewer double bookings across Booking.com, Airbnb and phone bookings

What we build for you

  • Online Booking Systems: direct booking with UPI deposits, WhatsApp confirmations and calendars linked to your booking-site listings
  • Websites & Local SEO: a fast website with a page for each room, boat or package, written for what travellers search
  • Posters & Video Ads: reels and photos edited for Instagram, where many travellers find stays
  • Meta & Google Ads: ads aimed at travellers planning trips to Kerala, and at past guests
  • AI Chat Assistants: an assistant that answers questions about rooms, food, check-in and getting there, at any hour

Questions

Should I leave Booking.com and Airbnb?

No. Booking sites bring guests who would never find you otherwise. The aim is to win more direct bookings from returning guests and people who already found you, while keeping your listings.

Can guests pay a deposit online?

Yes, by UPI, card or net banking through a payment gateway in your name, with the balance paid on arrival if you prefer.

Do you photograph the property?

We edit and design with your photos and videos, and can advise on what to shoot. Tell us if you need a photographer and we will discuss it.
