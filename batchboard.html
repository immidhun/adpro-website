Home / BatchBoard

Attendance, live on your centre TV.

₹5,000 one-time, no subscription

A lobby TV app for computer and coaching centres: live batch attendance on screen, your ads in between.

What the TV shows

  • Live batch boards: from 15 minutes before each batch starts until 5 minutes after, the TV shows who has checked in and who is on leave.
  • An opening brief when the centre opens for the day.
  • Between batches: calm music, your video ads and useful information for students and parents waiting in the lobby.

How attendance gets there

Tutors mark attendance from the BatchBoard app on their phone, and it appears on the TV at once. Leave can be marked too, so the board shows students on leave separately from those who have not arrived yet.

Made for a public lobby

  • Shows first name and initial only, never full names or phone numbers
  • Batch boards run Monday to Friday; weekends show your content loop
  • Styled to your centre's logo and brand colours
  • Runs on any Android TV or Google TV box plugged into the TV you already have

Price

₹5,000, one time. No monthly subscription. Includes set-up on your TV box, branding to your centre, and showing your staff how to use it.
