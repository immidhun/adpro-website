Questions

Are there rules on advertising for doctors and clinics?

Yes. Medical councils and advertising standards limit what doctors and health services can claim. We keep wording factual, avoid promises of cures or results, and check claims with you before anything is published.

Can patients book appointments online?

Yes. We can add an online booking system with slots for each doctor, or keep it simple with a WhatsApp booking button.

How do we get more Google reviews?

By asking every satisfied patient at the right moment, with a direct link to your review page. We set up the link and a simple routine for your front desk. We never write or buy reviews.
