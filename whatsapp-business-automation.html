Home / WhatsApp Automation

Answer every WhatsApp message, even at night.

Automatic WhatsApp replies on the official WhatsApp Business Platform. They quote your real prices and details, and step aside when your staff reply.

What it does

  • Replies straight away to common questions: prices, fees, timings, location and what to bring
  • Menus and buttons so customers can pick a product, course or service instead of typing
  • Collects the details you need, such as name, place and what they want, and saves them to a Google Sheet or your own system
  • Sends brochures, price lists and your location when asked
  • Alerts your staff when someone is ready to buy or book, so a person can call them
  • Goes quiet when your staff reply by hand, so customers never get a robot answer in the middle of a real conversation

Built on the official platform

We build on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (the Cloud API), not on unofficial bulk-sending tools that can get your number blocked. Messages you send first, such as reminders and follow-ups, use templates approved by Meta, and they go only to people who have contacted you or agreed to hear from you.

Meta charges for some types of message under its own pricing. We explain what that means for you before anything is switched on.

Reminders and follow-ups

  • Appointment, class and payment reminders
  • Order and delivery updates
  • A follow-up to enquiries that went quiet, sent once and politely
  • Offer messages to customers who opted in

A real example

For G-TEC Computer Education in Charummood we built a WhatsApp auto-reply engine. It quotes exactly the same course fees as the centre's website and goes quiet as soon as a staff member replies by hand. See the full project in our work.

Questions

Will my WhatsApp number get banned?

Bans usually come from unofficial bulk-messaging tools. We build on Meta's official platform and follow its rules on who you can message and when.

Can my staff still reply to customers themselves?

Yes. Staff can take over any conversation, and the automation steps aside as soon as they do.

Can I use my existing business number?

Usually, yes. We check how your number is set up now and explain the options before changing anything.

Can it send bulk messages to a list I bought?

No. WhatsApp only allows business messages to people who contacted you or agreed to hear from you, and breaking that rule puts your number at risk.
